# List Required Results and Rules

Treat the task statement as the primary source of truth. Inspect existing files and the run environment before implementation.

## Output

Use `templates/required-results-and-rules.md` to record:

- Explicit goals and deliverables.
- Functional and non-functional requirements.
- Constraints and prohibited actions.
- Confirmed facts and their source locations.
- Assumptions and their impact.
- Short, decision-focused questions for the business owner.
- Observable acceptance criteria and verification methods.

Do not expand product scope to resolve ambiguity. Before answers arrive, perform only safe inspection and structure discovery that does not depend on the unknown decision. Do not propose a design that requires an external service or another AI.
